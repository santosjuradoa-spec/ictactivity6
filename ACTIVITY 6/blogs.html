Journal notes for everyday matcha

Explore a few simple ways to prepare matcha and enjoy it in your next cup.

The basics

A simple whisk, a smoother cup

Sift matcha into a bowl, add a splash of warm water, and whisk in a quick zigzag until smooth. Add more water or milk to suit your cup.

Make it iced

Build an iced matcha latte

Whisk matcha with a little warm water first, then pour it over a glass of ice and your milk of choice. Stir and adjust it to your taste.

A coffee twist

Try a matcha-and-coffee pour

Add milk and ice to a glass, pour in prepared matcha, then finish with espresso. Stir together or leave the layers as they are.
